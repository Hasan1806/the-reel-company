The Reality

Content Is The Biggest
Growth Lever.
And The Hardest To Scale.

Most brands hit the same wall when trying to scale content. Here's what's holding you back.

01

Hiring in-house is too expensive

Building a content team means salaries, benefits, equipment, software, and management overhead — before you've filmed a single frame.

02

Freelancers are inconsistent

One great video, then silence. Freelancers deliver varying quality, miss deadlines, and disappear — leaving your pipeline dry when you need it most.

03

Agencies charge enterprise rates

Traditional production agencies quote $5k–$25k per video. That's not a content strategy — that's a quarterly budget burned on one asset.

04

Platforms demand constant volume

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Meta Ads all punish brands that post infrequently. The algorithm rewards volume AND quality — simultaneously.

05

UGC is hard to produce at scale

Authentic-feeling content requires the right creators, briefs, direction, and editing. Doing this consistently without a dedicated system is nearly impossible.
